Distinct GPU VRAM capacities of the last ~30 years of cards; only tiers that fit at least one measured model are listed (the smallest needs  GiB of whole-stack cost). The estimated w/s column uses the lowest-bandwidth card found at each capacity — conservative for anything faster.
VRAM fit uses the whole measured stack cost (weights + KV + buffers + runtime), conservatively taken from the CPU-side instrument — the GPU stack is expected to need less, so a fit here is a lower bound on what the card can hold.

How the recommendation is computed. Candidates are the seven qwen lineage members of the overnight sweep, each admitted on the v3.1 corpus wall (stall-rate verdict: PASS iff ≤ 5% of turns stall the reader; measured stall rates 0.0–2.6%, all PASS, 50 conversations / 267 turns each) — the same pool as the system-RAM lineage picker. A candidate is eligible iff (a) its measured whole-stack cost (weights + KV + buffers + runtime, MemAvailable-delta instrument) fits the card's VRAM — conservative, since the CPU-side cost is an upper bound on the GPU-side one — and (b) its predicted w/s at the card's bandwidth clears the 5.0 reader line, evaluated at each capacity's lowest-bandwidth card (conservative for anything faster). Eligibility is the VRAM fit; the w/s column shows the reader-line margin.

Two extrapolations, both flagged. The cost criterion carries the CPU measurement onto the GPU unchanged (conservative by design; the first GPU-side calibration run should replace it), and the linear bandwidth law is validated only on system RAM — on GPU, compute and kernel overheads can make the true w/s higher than predicted for small models (the law stays conservative) or the decode path can behave differently. Treat GPU recommendations as provisional until the study re-benches at least one candidate on a GPU.

VRAM figures are nominal card capacity; a small fraction is consumed by the display/OS. Bandwidth figures are the card's theoretical peak (memory type × bus). Quant is out of scope for this study: this tool answers the Q8_0 question only.
