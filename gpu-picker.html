How the recommendation is computed. Candidates are the measured members of the studied lineages (qwen, granite, MiniCPM, Phi), each scored under protocol v4.3 (session 34, addendum 33): a depth-scored ladder from the 4,096-token floor, doubling rungs, score = the deepest rung passing BOTH the speed gate (n=1, worst-turn ≥ 5 w/s, ≤ 5% stall) AND FWE (n=1, 3/3 coded words), refined to 1,024-token resolution — the same pool as the system-RAM picker. A candidate is eligible iff (a) its minimum RAM (whole-stack machine cost: weights + KV + buffers + runtime, MemAvailable-delta instrument, cold-cache at the scored rung) fits the card's VRAM — conservative, since the CPU-side cost is an upper bound on the GPU-side one — and (b) its predicted w/s at the card's bandwidth clears the 5.0 reader line, evaluated at each capacity's lowest-bandwidth card (conservative for anything faster). Eligibility is the VRAM fit; the w/s column shows the reader-line margin.

Two extrapolations, both flagged. The minimum-RAM criterion carries the CPU measurement onto the GPU unchanged (conservative by design; the first GPU-side calibration run should replace it), and the linear bandwidth law is validated only on system RAM — on GPU, compute and kernel overheads can make the true w/s higher than predicted for small models (the law stays conservative) or the decode path can behave differently. Treat GPU recommendations as provisional until the study re-benches at least one candidate on a GPU.

VRAM figures are nominal card capacity; a small fraction is consumed by the display/OS. Bandwidth figures are the card's theoretical peak (memory type × bus). Quant: each family appears once, at its best-scoring quant (Q4_K_M where the Q4 run scored deeper, Q8_0 otherwise; session 35, addendum 2).
