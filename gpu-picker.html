Which model fits your GPU's VRAM? This study measured nine small language-model families (0.8B to 3B parameters) doing real long-context work, then certified each one with a medal for quality, earned on measured runs: bronze = it passed at least once at that context size; silver = reliable — passed often enough that we are confident it usually works; gold = confident — passed often enough to trust on a bad day. The VRAM numbers are the measured whole-stack memory cost (weights + KV cache + buffers + runtime), taken from the CPU-side instrument — a conservative upper bound for the GPU stack. All measured models — the runs behind the medals

Every family the study measured, at every context size, with the raw pass counts out of 21 runs and the medal each rung earned. This is the data the recommendations come from — nothing hidden.

How the recommendation is computed. The study ran each model family 21 times per context size up a doubling ladder of context sizes (4k, 8k, 16k, … 256k context size). At each context size the model had to find hidden words in a long coded text — a pass means finding them. The raw pass counts earn the medals: bronze = at least one pass out of 21 (it can work, but expect misses); silver = reliable, roughly 13 or more passes out of 21 (we are confident it usually works); gold = confident, roughly 16 or more out of 21 (trust it on a bad day). The recommended model is the deepest silver-or-better one that (a) fits your RAM (measured whole-stack cost: weights + KV cache + buffers + runtime, cold) and (b) is predicted to stay above the 5 w/s reader line at your bandwidth (speed scales linearly with memory bandwidth: measured@102.4×BW/102.4). Two extrapolations, both flagged. The minimum-RAM criterion carries the CPU measurement onto the GPU unchanged (conservative by design; the first GPU-side calibration run should replace it), and the linear bandwidth law is validated only on system RAM — on GPU, compute and kernel overheads can make the true w/s higher than predicted for small models (the law stays conservative) or the decode path can behave differently. Treat GPU recommendations as provisional until the study re-benches at least one candidate on a GPU.

VRAM figures are nominal card capacity; a small fraction is consumed by the display/OS. Bandwidth figures are the card's theoretical peak (memory type × bus).
