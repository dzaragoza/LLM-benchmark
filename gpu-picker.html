Bandwidth is not the constraint on GPUs: the lowest-bandwidth card on the current market (224 GB/s) still predicts  w/s for the slowest measured candidate under the linear law — well over the 5.0 reader line — so the recommendation depends only on VRAM. VRAM fit uses the whole measured stack cost (weights + KV + buffers + runtime), conservatively taken from the CPU-side instrument — the GPU stack is expected to need less, so a fit here is a lower bound on what the card can hold.

How the recommendation is computed. Candidates are the seven qwen lineage members of the overnight sweep, each measured with the v3.1 instrument (stall-rate verdict: PASS iff ≤ 5% of turns stall the reader; measured stall rates 0.0–2.6%, all PASS, 50 conversations / 267 turns each) — the same pool as the system-RAM lineage picker. A candidate is eligible iff (a) its measured whole-stack cost (weights + KV + buffers + runtime, MemAvailable-delta instrument) fits the card's VRAM — conservative, since the CPU-side cost is an upper bound on the GPU-side one — and (b) its predicted w/s at the card's bandwidth clears the 5.0 reader line. Under that law even the lowest-bandwidth card on the current market (224 GB/s) predicts 13.8 w/s for the slowest candidate, so no card you can buy is bandwidth-limited for this pool and the recommendation depends only on VRAM.

Two extrapolations, both flagged. The cost criterion carries the CPU measurement onto the GPU unchanged (conservative by design; the first GPU-side calibration run should replace it), and the linear bandwidth law is validated only on system RAM — on GPU, compute and kernel overheads can make the true w/s higher than predicted for small models (the law stays conservative) or the decode path can behave differently. Treat GPU recommendations as provisional until the study re-benches at least one candidate on a GPU.

VRAM figures are nominal card capacity; a small fraction is consumed by the display/OS. Bandwidth figures are the card's theoretical peak (memory type × bus). Quant is out of scope for this study: this tool answers the Q8_0 question only.
