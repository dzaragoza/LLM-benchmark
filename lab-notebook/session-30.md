### Session 30, addendum 67 — the sentinel certificate pre-registered: the certificate form (a one-sided tolerance bound at the author's ruled s=2), S constructed (tokenizer class × size cone, probe-assigned), Δ = 0.05 w/t registered [M] with the qwen sentinel run as its exit plan, the Q8-only ruling (parameters self-select), the llama and phi Q8 cones issued from cal-pass data, the mistral negative cone, gemma ruled out of scope

**The author's question, formalized.** Given a set S of well-behaved models with an unknown w/s bottom b lying in an unknown band B, find a benchmark such that one measurement W at s sigma certifies which other models pass. The form: measure one SENTINEL s* of the set at W, with sampling error σ (the registered instrument: n=50 convs ≈ 267 turns, p05, 2σ(w/t) = ±0.078, addendum 60). The certificate over a promised set P holds iff (1) the set's bottom band falls inside the sentinel's CI — ε ≤ sσ, where ε is the TRANSFER error, how far below the sentinel another member's bottom may sit — and (2) W − sσ ≥ 5.0. Both hold iff s ∈ [ε/σ, (W−5.0)/σ]; the window is NON-EMPTY iff ε ≤ W − 5.0, an existence condition that contains no σ and no s: a badly chosen S cannot be rescued by benching, and instrument precision only moves WHERE the s-window sits, not WHETHER there is one. The converse edge is new: for fixed s the feasible σ-window is [ε/s, (W−5.0)/s] — the instrument can be TOO PRECISE (once σ < ε/s the CI no longer covers the set's band, and the measurement characterizes the sentinel alone) — addendum 60's "precision the predictor cannot use" now has a hard bound. The author ruled s = 2; it is consistent with the calibration's own grade by arithmetic, not luck (both were scaled to one rung of reader-rate resolution: at 12 t/s, 2σ ≈ ±0.9 w/s).

**S constructed — the set is tokenizer class × size cone.** S(class c, rung r, machine class M) = {models m : tokenizer(m) ∈ c, size_m ≤ size_sentinel, well-behaved}. Membership has two axes with two instruments. Class: strong form = tokenizer IDENTITY (same vocab + merges, checkable by hash — the same evidence the genetic-caveat log already uses: InternLM3's qwen-class tokenizer, deepseek-r1's Qwen-2.5 base, granite-3.3's llama-class vocab); weak form = tokenizer EFFICIENCY, probe-measured w/t within Δ/2 = 0.025 of a measured class center (tokenizer_probe.py, zero model cost — this is addendum 59's pre-shortlist role made structural: the probe can classify a model whose weights are never downloaded, which is what makes S constructible for hypothetical models). Size: the law (t/s monotone in size within the class, R² 0.9996) makes the largest member the cone's t/s-bottom, so domination below the sentinel is mechanical. The probe's own calibration evidence is on disk: probe-vs-generation-p05 gaps across the three usable families are qwen −0.078, mistral −0.004, phi −0.027 — Δ-scale, one-sided (prompt-side reads slightly high), with llama's row contaminated by the addendum-54 joke-turn artifact and unusable in both directions. Well-behaved generation is NOT probe-assignable (gemma probes 0.55–0.70 healthy on well-behaved turns and generates 0.03–0.29 word-sparse, addendum 46): the conditional stays in every certificate, word for word.

**Δ, and why it lives in w/t when the budget lives in w/s.** The prediction factorizes: w/s = t/s × w/t, where t/s is machine/size-owned (law-certified, the KNOWN term in the transfer) and w/t is the class property (the thing S shares, the ONLY unknown the sentinel's measurement carries to other members). Δ is therefore the operational definition of "same class": two members are co-classed iff their w/t bottoms agree to within Δ. A w/s-space band would entangle size with content (a fixed w/s allowance shrinks relative at high t/s — it would imply content risk scales with size, backwards, and exactly the gemma lesson falsifies it) and could not be graded by the sentinel run (a w/s comparison would be contaminated by the already-known t/s difference). Δ converts to the budget ONCE per cone, at the sentinel's t/s — the conservative point, since a smaller member's higher t/s partially offsets a Δ-lower w/t: ε = Δ·t/s(s*) + law residual. Value 0.05 w/t, borrowed from the addendum-59 grading band (the largest same-family re-measurement move observed and still graded "held": mistral −0.004, phi −0.027, qwen −0.078 UPDATE at n=50). Registered [M], honestly: the grading band was designed for re-measuring the SAME member across instruments; the certificate needs it for cross-member transfer, and the study has NO clean cross-member datum (llama-3.1-8B's 0.144 vs llama-3.2-3B's 0.491 is contaminated by the addendum-54 degeneracy in both directions — unusable). Exit plan: the qwen sentinel run below — the 4B's cal p05 vs the 9B-calibrated 0.412 is the study's first cross-member transfer measurement; Δ VALIDATED iff the gap ≤ 0.05, else the measured gap re-derives the band and the transfer caveat becomes a registry finding.

**The study's shape (the cold start — the author's "how would this study begin?").** S cannot be created first and does not need to be; the phases are the ordinary study's phases, renamed. **A — the law:** any popular model's quant ladder on the small corpus (~25 min) fits BW_eff and t_inf; content-blind (a misconfigured model still streams at law speed), so no well-behavedness is needed here. **B — the anchors:** the n=50 calibration pass on each family being benched anyway (~30–40 min/family) yields the w/t p05s; THE CLASSES ARE DISCOVERED HERE, as measured clusters — and well-behavedness is certified here or the family is excluded by measurement (gemma's path). **C — S exists:** arithmetic, free (classes × law define the cones; from here the probe MAINTAINS membership but could never have created the first anchor). **D — the sentinel:** self-selects (below) and is benched once per class (~30–40 min) — the run audits the cone, grades Δ, and measures member transfer, all at once. **E — issue:** positive, negative (an empty cone is a valid, cheap certificate — predictor-only), or pending. Day one: nothing is predictable (a prediction without A+B constants is the anti-conservative 5-conv PASS trap, addenda 23/66), and the first sentinel is simply whichever model phase B benched — named sentinel afterward. Cold-start price for a NEW machine class: one ladder + one cal pass per class ≈ 1.5–3 h arms everything; every model after that is probe-assigned in seconds, and only trust-band straddlers and sentinels ever touch the bench. The 102.4-class replay is complete through D for two cones from the archived record — the certificate machinery added no new study; it reorganized the one already run.
