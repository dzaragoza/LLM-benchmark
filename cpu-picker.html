How the recommendation is computed. Candidates are the measured members of the studied lineages (qwen, granite, MiniCPM, Phi), each scored under protocol v4.3 (session 34, addendum 33): a depth-scored ladder that starts at the 4,096-token floor (addendum 32) and doubles the context rung until the model fails — the deepest rung at which it BOTH serves the reader (speed gate: n=1 conversation, worst-turn ≥ 5 w/s, ≤ 5% stall rate) AND counts (FWE: n=1, 3/3 coded words) is the score, refined to 1,024-token resolution by binary search. A candidate is eligible iff (a) its minimum RAM (whole-stack machine cost: weights + KV + buffers + runtime, MemAvailable-delta instrument, cold-cache at the scored rung) fits your RAM, and (b) its predicted w/s at your bandwidth clears the 5.0 reader line. The w/s anchor is the worst turn at the model's deepest speed-measured rung. Bandwidth scaling is the pre-registered halving form: w/s(BW) = w/smeasured@102.4 × BW/102.4 — linear because at these sizes decode is bandwidth-dominated; for the smallest members it is conservative. The recommendation is the highest-depth eligible candidate.

Out of the pool. Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct is out (counting floor — it cannot do FWE at any measured depth); Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct is out (task refusal; the benchmark is not changed to fit a model). Models scoring zero under v4.3 rank last and cannot be recommended. Quant: each family appears once, at its best-scoring quant (Q4_K_M where the Q4 run scored deeper, Q8_0 otherwise; session 34, addendum 43).

Caveats: w/s figures are the worst turn at each model's deepest speed-measured rung (the v4.3 ladder's n=1 screen — one conversation at the ranked depth). Depth scores are n=1 screening ranks (session 34, addendum 33). The bandwidth law is validated only at the 102.4 GB/s class. Minimum-RAM values are run-shaped: treat the fit boundary as soft within ~1 GiB.
